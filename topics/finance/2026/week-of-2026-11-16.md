## Financial stability risks from large IPOs, equity issuance and passive investing

Published: November 18, 2026

The ECB examines how large IPOs, AI-related equity issuance and passive investing affect financial stability. It says issuance can absorb losses and reduce firm-level leverage, but peaks with investor exuberance, weakens in downturns and may amplify market cyclicality as passive funds increase demand and financing becomes more dependent on investor flows.

**Why it matters:** The ECB concludes that these developments do not currently pose a systemic risk, but they could make equity financing more sensitive to shifts in sentiment and amplify financial cycles.

Source: [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/special/html/ecb.fsrart202611_03~46e116aeb8.en.html) · Based on feed excerpt.

## Monetary policy, segmented capital markets and innovation

Published: November 17, 2026

This ECB working paper, not presented as peer-reviewed research, uses granular euro-area firm balance-sheet and patent data matched with bank-level and credit-registry information. It finds that monetary tightening constrains innovation most for bank-dependent, intangible-intensive and AI-sector firms, while diversified equity access cushions the effect.

**Why it matters:** The study identifies capital-market segmentation as a mechanism linking monetary policy to innovation, implying that broader access to finance could reduce the innovation drag of tighter policy.

Source: [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3311~66cc2a4d65.en.pdf) · Based on feed excerpt.

Tags: ECB, monetary policy, innovation, AI, bank credit, research
