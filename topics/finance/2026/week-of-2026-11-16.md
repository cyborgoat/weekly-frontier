## Monetary policy, segmented capital markets and innovation

Published: November 17, 2026

This ECB working paper, not presented as peer-reviewed research, uses granular euro-area firm balance-sheet and patent data matched with bank-level and credit-registry information. It finds that monetary tightening constrains innovation most for bank-dependent, intangible-intensive and AI-sector firms, while diversified equity access cushions the effect.

**Why it matters:** The study identifies capital-market segmentation as a mechanism linking monetary policy to innovation, implying that broader access to finance could reduce the innovation drag of tighter policy.

Source: [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3311~66cc2a4d65.en.pdf) · Based on feed excerpt.

Tags: ECB, monetary policy, innovation, AI, bank credit, research

## Transatlantic securitisation gap revisited: comparing EU and US securitisation architectures

Published: November 19, 2026

The ECB revisited the claimed transatlantic securitisation gap and found that common comparisons omit EU covered-bond issuance. Including covered bonds and applying an economy-scaled asset mobilisation ratio points to shifting issuance patterns and narrowing convergence rather than a structurally underdeveloped EU market.

**Why it matters:** The study demonstrates how instrument definitions and denominator choices can materially change conclusions about Europe’s capital-market development.

Source: [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpops/ecb.op403.en.pdf) · Based on feed excerpt.
