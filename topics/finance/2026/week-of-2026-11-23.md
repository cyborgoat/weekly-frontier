## The geography of financial frictions

Published: November 23, 2026

This unreviewed ECB working paper uses proprietary Survey on the Access to Finance of Enterprises data to document persistent regional differences in European firms’ access to external finance and models them in a 22-country monetary union.

**Why it matters:** The paper finds that high-friction economies respond less to monetary shocks and face a costlier output-inflation trade-off, while financial integration improves monetary-policy effectiveness at the expense of financial stability.
