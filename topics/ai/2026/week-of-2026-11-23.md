## GLM-5.4: Toward Generalized Coding with Our First Loop Transformer

Published: November 23, 2026

Simon Willison reports that Chinese AI lab Z.ai released GLM-5.4, its first model based on a looped transformer architecture in which weight-shared layers refine hidden states over multiple iterations. He tested it through Z.ai's OpenAI-compatible API and compared generated pelican images across model generations.

**Why it matters:** The release adds another recently reported loop-transformer approach to AI coding and reasoning. Its significance is tempered by the model remaining unavailable as open weights and requiring special API access; Willison's visual comparisons are informal observations rather than a formal evaluation.

Source: [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/23/glm-54/) · Based on feed excerpt.

## Many languages, one common neural code

Published: November 23, 2026

MIT News summarizes a multilingual AI study of speakers of 21 languages that found evidence the human brain may represent meaning in a broadly similar way across languages.

**Why it matters:** If supported by the underlying study, the findings suggest a language-independent aspect to human meaning that could inform the design and interpretation of multilingual AI systems.

## OpenAI and Cisco introduce Shirudo

Published: November 23, 2026

OpenAI and Cisco introduced Shirudo, an open-source Linux runtime designed to help teams protect running systems while they deploy patches.

**Why it matters:** Shirudo gives organizations an open-source tool for maintaining and securing live systems during patch rollouts, a sensitive area of production infrastructure.
