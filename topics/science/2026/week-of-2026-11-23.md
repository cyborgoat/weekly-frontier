## NASA Seeks Partners to Design First Nuclear Reactor for the Moon

Published: November 23, 2026

NASA is seeking industry partners to develop and test Lunar Reactor-1, a small fission power system designed to generate 20 kilowatts of electricity on the Moon.

**Why it matters:** The project is a major step toward sustained human activity on the lunar surface and brings industry into the development of lunar nuclear power systems.

## NASA’s Webb Shows How Supernova Dust Chronicles Star Evolution

Published: November 23, 2026

NASA’s James Webb Space Telescope used high-resolution, mid-infrared observations to image the gas and dust in the supernova remnant Cassiopeia A.

**Why it matters:** The observations provide a detailed view of a massive star’s explosive aftermath and help explain how supernova remnants contribute material that can seed new stars and planets.

## Studying Cancer Cells in Microgravity

Published: November 23, 2026

**Why it matters:** The results could support new testing for predicting metastasis risk and more personalized approaches to cancer care.
