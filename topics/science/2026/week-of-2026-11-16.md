## Closest relatives of poxviruses prevail in human and animal guts worldwide

Published: November 17, 2026

A Nature study describes egoviruses, a diversified group of large, linear-DNA viruses forming a putative new order most closely related to animal-infecting poxviruses. The study reports that they prevail in human and animal guts worldwide.

**Why it matters:** The discovery broadens recognized viral diversity and identifies a distinct lineage close to poxviruses, providing a new reference for research into viral evolution and ecology.

Source: [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11283-z) · Based on feed excerpt.

Published: November 19, 2026

**Why it matters:** The milestone establishes a new distance benchmark for humanity’s farthest-flying spacecraft and underscores the probe’s exceptional longevity.
