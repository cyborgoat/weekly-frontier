## The Stormy Southwest Pacific

Published: November 19, 2026

NASA’s Earth Observatory reports that Cyclone Xavier rapidly intensified near the Cook Islands to peak winds of 220 km/h, becoming the strongest November cyclone east of the International Date Line on record, according to hurricane expert Phil Klotzbach. **Why it matters:** Xavier illustrates how El Niño can shift South Pacific cyclone activity eastward and favour rapid intensification, increasing regional preparedness stakes.
