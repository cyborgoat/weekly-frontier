## ‘Light and sound’ therapy for Alzheimer’s flops in long-anticipated trial

Published: November 16, 2026

Nature reports that a closely watched trial found no cognitive benefit from a headset combining light and sound stimulation to treat Alzheimer’s disease.

**Why it matters:** The null result tempers expectations for the headset as an Alzheimer’s treatment and underscores that proposed interventions must demonstrate clinical benefit.

## Voyager 1 is about to achieve a cosmic first: one ‘light day’ from Earth

Published: November 16, 2026

**Why it matters:** The milestone highlights the probe’s exceptional longevity and its continued ability to return scientific data under severe power constraints.
